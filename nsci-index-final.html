Mission, Vision & Values

🎯 Our Mission

  • Raise student concerns through representation.
  • Promote quality education and knowledge.
  • Support student welfare.
  • Encourage equal opportunities.
  • Promote safe and respectful environments.
  • Develop leadership and innovation.
  • Encourage unity and cooperation.
  • Promote positive social contribution.

◉ Our Vision

To build an educated, united and progressive student community where every student has the opportunity to learn, participate, lead and contribute positively to society.
